# Australian sources

## OAIC NDB aggregates

The current reviewed Data.gov.au XLSX is a periodic national aggregate. It
reports notifications received by the OAIC and includes month, cause, sector,
affected-population, identification, and notification-timing dimensions.
Source-reported worldwide and Australian population scopes stay separate.
Percentages retain estimated or rounded state. OAIC statistics are based on
self-reported information and may be revised; historical value changes are
recorded without creating named incidents.

## NSW MNDB aggregate snapshot

The adapter follows the IPC reporting index to the current six-month snapshot
and extracts only the reviewed sector table. It does not transcribe charts or
complete PDF text. The state scheme, sectors, and reporting period differ from
the national OAIC data.

## NSW Public Notifications Register

Register rows name agencies and display incident and public-notification dates.
This selective rolling public window is not all breaches notified to the IPC.
Links generally remain public for at least 12 months. Expiry is not remediation.

## OAIC regulatory actions

A reviewed manifest distinguishes preliminary inquiry, investigation,
enforceable undertaking, determination, civil proceeding allegation, judgment,
penalty order, closure, and no-adverse-finding outcomes. A proceeding contains
allegations unless and until an outcome source says otherwise. Aggregate NDB
records do not name organizations and cannot become regulatory or incident
records.
